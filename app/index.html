Roadmap to the Platinum

Missable trophies: one, possibly

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy PowerPyx and GTABase flag as possibly missable. The first meditation — Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house, after the mission Down to Earth — is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. Do it in Chapter 1 and the rest of the list is safe: there is no mission replay, but free-roam after the story keeps everything else reachable.
